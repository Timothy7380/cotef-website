Get to Know Us

The Complete Teenagers Empowerment Foundation (COTEF) is a faith-driven non-profit organisation dedicated to raising wholesome teenagers who love and know God, and equipping them to positively impact society. Founded on May 12th, 2021 as The Complete Teen (“Nurturing Young Minds”), we rebranded as COTEF in 2025. We educate and sensitise teenagers on spirituality, mental and emotional well-being, character development, financial literacy and relationship skills.

Our Vision

A generation of well-rounded teenagers who know God, love Him, and are impacting their world positively.

Our Mission

Nurturing teenagers with the right and adequate knowledge needed to properly develop every area of their lives.

Frequent Questions

Complete Teenagers Empowerment Foundation (COTEF) is a registered Non-Governmental Organisation based in Ikorodu, Lagos. We nurture teenagers with the Truth and help them grow to love God, know Him and impact their world positively.

We were formerly known as The Complete Teen and rebranded as COTEF in 2025.

The Academy runs a monthly series of mentorship sessions (past series include Choices, What’s Trending, Gifts and Love Matters) covering spirituality, finances, career, academics, character, relationships and mental wellbeing. It also includes book reviews, affirmations and a supportive community of teens and mentors.

ESOHE is COTEF’s Educational Support Outreach. We give secondary school students free learning packs and self-help books, hold inspiring talks and breakout sessions, and offer parents health checks and counselling. The 2025 edition reached 200+ students in Baiyeku; ESOHE 2026 (“Identity”) targeted 300+ students in Igbogbo, Ikorodu.
